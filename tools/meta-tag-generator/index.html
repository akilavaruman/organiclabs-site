      2026 Basics
      
Which Meta Tags Still Matter in 2026?

      
Most meta tags in old generators do nothing. Six do real work.

      
A meta tag generator should give you the tags that change something and nothing else. Many popular generators still ask for meta keywords, revisit-after, rating and distribution. Google stopped using meta keywords in 2009, and the others never did anything in Google Search.

      
The tags that matter fall into three groups. Search tags (title, description, canonical tag, robots) shape how Google shows and indexes the page. Social tags (Open Graph and the Twitter card) control link previews. Technical tags (charset and viewport) make the page render correctly. Google keeps a list of the meta tags it supports, along with the ones it ignores.

Why Google Rewrites Your Title and Description (and How to Reduce It)

      
Google rewrites most snippets. Write ones it has no reason to replace.

      
The data. Ahrefs studied 20,000 keywords and found Google rewrites meta descriptions 62.78% of the time. Zyppy looked at 80,959 titles and found 61% were rewritten at least in part. Very short and very long titles were rewritten over 95% of the time. Titles with brackets were rewritten 77.6% of the time.

      
What reduces rewrites.

      
    
        
  • Keep the title close to the page's H1.
    • 
        
  • Stay within 50 to 60 characters.
    • 
        
  • Use a dash as the brand separator rather than a pipe.
    • 
        
  • Write a description that answers the query the page targets, not a slogan.
    • 
      

      
Why it matters more now. Our 39-study AI Overviews meta-analysis found a 47.5% median drop in clicks when an AI Overview appears. Fewer clicks are left, so your listing has to earn the ones that remain. The title and description are the two lines you control.

      
"You can't stop Google rewriting your snippet. You can give it fewer reasons to."

    


    

      AI Search
      
Do Meta Tags Matter for AI Search?

      
Less than people claim, more than nothing.

      
No published study shows that meta descriptions change whether ChatGPT, Perplexity or AI Overviews cite a page. We would rather say that plainly than guess.

      
Three things are clear. The title and canonical tag help any system identify the page and its preferred URL. Open Graph tags power link previews inside chat apps and Slack, where your links get shared. And AI crawlers read raw HTML only. The Vercel and MERJ crawl study found GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot don't run JavaScript, so tags injected by a script are invisible to them.

      
For what does correlate with AI citations, see our 19-study citation signals meta-analysis. Structured, answer-first content scored highest.

    


    

      Writing
      
How to Write a Meta Title and Meta Description That Get Clicked

      
Specifics beat adjectives.

      
These rules match the warnings in the tool.

      
    
        
  • Title: put the primary keyword near the start, keep it to 50 to 60 characters, match the meaning of the H1, and put the brand at the end.
    • 
        
  • Description: use 140 to 160 characters, say what the page gives the reader, include the keyword once, and skip double quote marks (they can cut the tag short).
    • 
        
  • Unique per page: no boilerplate repeated across a template.
    • 
        
  • Numbers over adjectives: "SOC 2 in 6 weeks" beats "fast, easy compliance".
    • 
      

      
Here is a B2B SaaS feature page, before and after:

      

        
Before
Acme | The Best-in-Class Compliance Platform | Features
Acme is the leading, fast and easy compliance platform trusted by innovative teams everywhere to achieve their goals.

        
After
SOC 2 Compliance Automation in 6 Weeks - Acme
Automate SOC 2 evidence across AWS, GitHub and Okta. Most teams reach audit readiness in 6 weeks. See pricing and integrations.

      

      
The first title leads with the brand and an adjective. The second leads with the query and a number. The first description could describe any product. The second tells the searcher what they get.

    


    

      Social
      
Open Graph and Twitter Card Tags: Get Link Previews Right

      
One image, five tags, absolute URLs.

      
Every link preview needs five Open Graph tags: og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url and og:type. Use a 1200 x 630 image and keep key text in the centre, because some platforms crop the edges. The og:image must be a full https:// URL; relative paths break previews.

      
Old previews often stick around because platforms cache them. After you fix your tags, refresh the cache with the LinkedIn Post Inspector or the Facebook Sharing Debugger. Slack refreshes on its own schedule, so a re-share may still show the old card for a while.

      
For X, add twitter:card (summary_large_image for a big image) and your handles. X falls back to Open Graph for title, description and image, so you rarely need separate Twitter tags.

      FAQs
      
FAQs

      
What is a meta tag generator?
A meta tag generator turns a short form (title, description, image, URL) into the HTML tags that go in a page's <head>. This one also shows a live Google preview and outputs canonical, robots and optional Open Graph tags in one block.

      
Which meta tags are most important for SEO?
The title tag and meta description matter most, followed by canonical and robots when you need them. Open Graph and Twitter tags control link previews on social and chat apps. Meta keywords does nothing at Google.

      
Does Google still use the meta keywords tag?
No. Google stopped using meta keywords for ranking in 2009 and lists it as unsupported. You can delete it.

      
Why does Google rewrite my meta description?
Google shows the text it thinks best answers the search. Ahrefs found it rewrites descriptions about 63% of the time. Descriptions that match the page's main query and stay within length are used more often.

      
How long should a title tag and meta description be?
Aim for 50 to 60 characters for titles and about 150 to 160 for descriptions. Google truncates by pixel width, so check long titles with our meta description checker.

      
Why isn't my Open Graph image showing?
Usually the og:image URL is relative, blocked, too small or cached. Use a full https:// URL, a 1200x630 image, then refresh the preview with the platform's debugger.

      
Do meta tags help with AI search like ChatGPT?
There's no evidence meta descriptions change AI citations. Titles and canonicals help identify the page, and tags must be in the raw HTML because AI crawlers don't run JavaScript.

      
Is this meta tag generator free?
Yes. No sign-up. The form, previews and code all run in your browser.
