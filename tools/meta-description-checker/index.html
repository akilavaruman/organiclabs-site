Length

How Long Should a Meta Description Be in 2026?

There's no official limit. There is a point where Google cuts you off.

Google publishes no maximum length for meta descriptions. Its snippet documentation says snippets are truncated as needed to fit the device width. So every number you see online is someone's measurement, not a rule.

That's why length checkers disagree. Some count characters and some measure pixels. Desktop and mobile results have different widths. And the limits shift every time Google changes its layout. A tool that says "160 characters" and a tool that says "920 pixels" can both be roughly right for the same description.

These are the safe ranges this checker uses. We recheck them against live results every quarter.

Put the key message in the first 120 characters. Portent's study found mobile results show about 118 to 121 characters of a description, and fewer when a date appears. Anything after that is a desktop bonus.

Write for the mobile cut. Treat the rest as extra.

Pixels

Why Pixels Beat Characters

Two titles can have the same length and very different widths.

Google's result box has a fixed width, and letters don't. A "W" is about four times wider than an "i". Capitals, numbers and wide letters fill the space faster than lowercase text. Research

Why Google Rewrites Your Meta Description (and Title)

You can't stop rewrites. You can give Google fewer reasons.

Descriptions. Ahrefs studied 20,000 keywords and found Google rewrites meta descriptions 62.78% of the time. Staying within length limits barely changed that rate. So length decides how your description displays, not whether Google uses it.

Titles. Zyppy looked at 80,959 titles and found 61% were rewritten at least in part. Very short and very long titles were rewritten over 95% of the time. Titles with brackets were rewritten 77.6% of the time.

What helps. Write a description that answers the page's main query. Keep the title close to the H1. Cut boilerplate like "Welcome to". Make sure the page has one description tag, in the raw HTML.

Why it matters more now. AI Overviews cut clicks by a median 47.5% across 39 studies in our meta-analysis. Your result may sit below an AI answer, with fewer clicks left to win. Writing

How to Write a Meta Description That Fits and Gets Clicked

Outcome first, specifics second, no filler.

  • Lead with the outcome the reader gets.
  • Include the keyword once, naturally.
  • Add one specific: a number, a timeframe or an audience.
  • End with what the reader will find on the page.
  • Avoid double quote marks. They can cut the tag short.

Here is a B2B SaaS feature page, before and after:

Before (171 characters)

Welcome to Acme! We are the leading, best-in-class compliance automation platform trusted by innovative teams worldwide. Click here to learn more about our "amazing" features.

After (126 characters)

Automate SOC 2 evidence across AWS, GitHub and Okta. Most teams reach audit readiness in 6 weeks. See pricing and integrations.

The first version wastes its opening on "Welcome to", runs past the desktop cut and contains quote marks. The second puts the outcome, a number and the next step inside the mobile limit.

How to Check Meta Descriptions Across Your Whole Site

Fix what's missing and duplicated before what's long.

FAQs

FAQs

What is a meta description checker?

A meta description checker measures your title and description against the space Google shows and tells you if they'll be cut off. This one measures pixel width, shows desktop and mobile previews, and checks a whole CSV at once.

How long should a meta description be?

About 120 to 155 characters, or up to roughly 920 pixels on desktop. Google has no official limit, so put the key message in the first 120 characters to survive on mobile.

How long should a title tag be?

Aim for 50 to 60 characters, or under about 600 pixels. Very short and very long titles are rewritten by Google most often.

Why does Google truncate by pixels, not characters?

Search results have a fixed width, and letters differ in width. A title with wide letters and capitals fills the space faster than one with narrow letters.

Why is Google not showing my meta description?

Google rewrites descriptions when page text matches the search better. Ahrefs found this happens about 63% of the time. Write a specific description that answers the page's main query.

Is meta description length a ranking factor?

No. Meta descriptions don't affect rankings. Length affects how your snippet displays and whether it reads well, which affects clicks.

Can I check meta descriptions in bulk?

Yes. Paste or upload a CSV of up to 500 rows from Screaming Frog, Search Console or Ahrefs. You get truncation, duplicate and missing flags, and a downloadable report.

Is this meta description checker free?

Yes. No sign-up. Single and bulk checks run in your browser, so your data stays on your device.
