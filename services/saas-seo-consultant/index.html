FAQ

SaaS SEO consultant FAQs

What does a SaaS SEO consultant do?

A SaaS SEO consultant sets your search strategy, decides which pages to build or fix, writes briefs and technical specs, reviews your team's work and tracks pipeline from search. Your team handles execution. Ours also covers AI search in ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews.

How much does a SaaS SEO consultant cost?

Independent consultants often charge $100 to $250 an hour, and monthly consulting retainers commonly run $3,000 to $10,000. Our consulting starts at $4,000 a month, or $200 an hour for ad hoc work.

Should we hire an SEO consultant or an SEO agency?

Hire a consultant if you have writers and developers who can execute and you need senior direction. Hire an agency if you don't have execution capacity. We offer both, and you can switch from consulting to full-service later.

What's the difference between a search optimization consultant and an SEO consultant?

Mostly the name. Today, search optimization also means AI search: being cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, not just ranking. Our consulting covers both.

How much of our team's time does consulting take?

Plan for about an hour a week for calls and reviews, plus the writing and dev work your team already does. We write the briefs and specs, so your team spends its time shipping.

Who will we actually work with?

One senior consultant on every call, from day one. No junior handoff. Our AI agents handle analysis and first drafts of briefs, and every output is reviewed before you see it.

How long until we see results?

Quick wins, like fixes and refreshes, can show within 1 to 3 months. Compounding gains in rankings, AI citations and pipeline usually take 6 to 12 months, depending on how fast your team ships.

Do you help with AI search (AEO and GEO)?

Yes, it's built into every engagement. We publish original research on how AI tools choose sources, including a 12,000-run study of LLM answers, and use it to plan your prompts, pages and mentions.

Can we switch to done-for-you later?

Yes. Many teams start with consulting and move to our Growth plan when they want more output. Your roadmap, data and agents carry over.

Do you only work with SaaS companies?

We focus on B2B SaaS and B2B tech. Our roadmaps, page types and pipeline reporting are built for considered, multi-touch buying journeys.

Is there a minimum contract?

A three-month sprint, then month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

What happens to the work if we stop?

You keep everything: roadmap, briefs, data, knowledge base and the AI agents we built for your team.
