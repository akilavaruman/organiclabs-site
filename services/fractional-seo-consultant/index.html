FAQ

Fractional SEO consultant FAQs

What is a fractional SEO consultant?

A fractional SEO consultant is a senior SEO lead who works part-time inside your team. They own the strategy, direct the work and report on results, like an in-house head of SEO, without a full-time salary. Ours also owns AI search.

How is fractional SEO different from SEO consulting?

A consultant advises and your team executes. A fractional lead owns the channel: sits in your team, manages the people and work, and is accountable for the number. Ours also brings AI agents that do much of the execution. See our SaaS SEO consulting if you only need direction.

How is a fractional SEO consultant different from an agency?

You get one senior person embedded in your team, not an account manager relaying to juniors. Ours combines that lead with an AI agent team, so you get agency-level output with in-house-level ownership.

How much does a fractional SEO consultant cost?

Fractional SEO leads commonly charge $3,500 to $10,000 a month, depending on scope and whether execution is included. Ours starts at $7,500 a month with execution through our AI agents included.

How many hours a week does a fractional SEO lead work?

Expect a weekly working session, regular team meetings and daily Slack access, plus the agent-driven execution in between. We agree the cadence at kick-off.

Who executes the work?

Our AI agents produce analysis, briefs, drafts and QA, and your fractional lead reviews everything. We publish directly or hand to your writers and developers, depending on how your team works.

Does fractional SEO include AI search (AEO and GEO)?

Yes. Your lead owns AI search: prompt mapping, visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI measured over repeated runs, and a plan for the sources AI cites.

How long until we see results?

Fixes and refreshes can show within 1 to 3 months. Compounding gains in rankings, AI citations and pipeline usually take 6 to 12 months.

When should we hire a full-time head of SEO instead?

When SEO is a proven channel and you need someone full-time on it, usually once the roadmap needs daily ownership. We'll help you hire and hand everything over.

Can you help us hire our in-house SEO lead?

Yes. We help write the role, review candidates and hand over the roadmap, data and agents to your new hire.

Is there a minimum contract?

A three-month sprint, then month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

What do we keep if we stop?

Everything: roadmap, content, data, knowledge base, and the AI agents we built for your team.
