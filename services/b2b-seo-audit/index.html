FAQ

B2B SEO audit FAQs

What is a B2B SEO audit?

A B2B SEO audit reviews your site's technical health, content, links and search visibility, then tells you what to fix to win more qualified traffic and pipeline. Ours also measures how AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity see and cite you.

What does your B2B SEO audit include?

Six areas: technical SEO, AI crawler access, content and page types, keyword and prompt gaps, backlinks and brand mentions, and AI visibility across 5 platforms. You get a ranked roadmap, an AI baseline, dev tickets, a content plan and a walk-through call.

How much does a B2B SEO audit cost?

Ours is a fixed price, agreed before we start. Comprehensive B2B audits commonly cost $5,000 to $15,000, depending on site size and depth.

How long does an SEO audit take?

Ours takes 15 days from access to walk-through call. Very large, multi-language or multi-product sites can take longer; we'll say so on the first call.

Does the audit cover AI search (AEO and GEO)?

Yes. We run your buyers' prompts repeatedly across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI, check whether AI crawlers can read your pages, and show which sources AI cites in your category.

What is an AI visibility audit?

An AI visibility audit measures how often AI tools mention or cite your brand for the prompts your buyers use, who they name instead and which sources they rely on. We report it as a range, because AI answers vary between runs.

What's a technical SEO audit, and is it included?

A technical SEO audit checks whether search engines and AI crawlers can crawl, render and index your pages. It's one of the six areas in our audit, delivered as dev-ready tickets.

How is this different from a free tool or a ChatGPT audit?

Tools list issues; they don't know which ones matter for your pipeline. A senior strategist ranks fixes by impact on Google, AI and revenue, and measures AI visibility across repeated runs rather than one answer.

What access do you need?

Read access to Google Search Console, GA4 and your CRM, plus the site URL. CMS access only if you want us to implement fixes.

Can you fix the issues you find?

Yes. Continue into a Strategy or Growth plan and we implement. Or we brief your developers and writers.

Can I buy an audit without a retainer?

Yes. The audit is a fixed-price, one-off project. No ongoing commitment.

Who does the audit?

A senior strategist, with AI agents handling data pulls and prompt runs. The person who does the analysis runs your walk-through call.
