FAQ

Services FAQs

Which Organic Labs service is right for us?

If you want everything handled, choose full-service SEO. If you need content, choose SEO content marketing. If you rank but AI tools recommend competitors, choose AI search optimization. If nobody owns SEO, choose a fractional lead. If your team executes and needs direction, choose consulting. Not sure yet? Start with an audit.

Do all services include AI search?

Yes. Every service covers Google and AI search, because our research found the two reward different things. AI visibility is always measured across repeated runs, not a single screenshot.

How much do your services cost?

Retainers start at $4,000 a month for Strategy, $7,500 for Growth and $10,995 for Scale. Every plan gets the same senior strategist. The B2B SEO audit is a fixed-fee, one-off project.

Who does the work?

A senior strategist owns your account and checks every piece of work. A team of AI agents, built for your company at kickoff, does the analysis, drafting, audits and builds.

Do you only work with B2B SaaS?

We focus on B2B SaaS and B2B tech, with long buying cycles and demos or signups as the goal. If you run a local business or an ecommerce store, a specialist will serve you better.

Is there a minimum contract?

Retainers start with a three-month sprint, then run month to month with 30 days' notice. The audit is a one-off project with no ongoing commitment.

Can we switch services later?

Yes. Many teams start with an audit or consulting and move to Growth or Scale when they want more output. Your roadmap, data and agents carry over.
